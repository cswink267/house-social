# Later — do not start

Chris, Oct 22 2026. These are extras. None of them are started. The order is locked. One at a time, when he asks. Do not pull the next one forward.

Ingest history, then make the knowledge visible and correctable, then apply it while writing, then capture reusable formats and explicit feedback, then, only if he still wants it, look across brands.

A missing number stays missing. Nothing is sent. Tokens stay in the vault. A comparison still needs three on both sides and a real gap. Do not invent a percent, a popularity score, or a confidence number the posts did not earn.

## 1. Historical Content Ingest Center

Highest priority extra. A dedicated flow for pulling in past content and metrics so the brand starts with a real baseline instead of learning only from new posts.

- Guided import: CSV, Excel, JSON, or a platform export (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, and the rest). Manual bulk paste is the fallback.
- Field mapping so a messy export can still match caption, date, platform, likes, comments, shares, saves, impressions, reach, video views, and the rest.
- Read-only API connectors later, for an ongoing sync of new posts and metrics. Not in the first slice.
- Cleaning: deduplicate, normalize dates, leave missing metrics missing, flag incomplete rows.
- An analysis pass right after import: group posts by performance, and extract patterns that are actually clear (hook, topic, format, time, length, media type).
- A First Insights report from that import: content types, hooks, times and days, pillars already in the data, and voice refinements only where the posts support them.
- Partial imports can be resumed. An audit log records what was imported and when, so the age and completeness of the history are known.
- Recent posts can be weighted higher only when the dates are real. Do not invent a confidence level. Say how many posts the line is based on.

This is the on-ramp. Voice, prediction, formats, and ideas should use this history instead of a generic pattern.

## 2. Memory / Context Panel

A transparent view of what is currently held for the brand, so it can be understood, trusted, and corrected.

- Summary of what the posts have already shown: hooks, formats, times, pillars, engagement by type, CTAs, voice patterns. Each line names the count it came from.
- If the count is thin, say it is thin. Do not dress it up as a confidence score.
- Editable: promote, demote, or delete a learned pattern. "This hook style is not actually preferred" has to stick.
- A timeline of what changed after an import or after a post that was actually ahead.
- "Why this recommendation?" from the composer or the calendar opens the part of the panel that produced it.
- Reset learned patterns without deleting the raw posts.
- Export the current memory for backup or review. The export stays redacted. No token, no machine path.

User corrections are the signal. The panel is the control surface. It does not get to hide a pattern that was deleted.

## 3. Performance Prediction Score

A visible range on a draft that reflects similarity to this brand's past posts. Build it only after the history exists.

- In the composer: Low, Medium, or High, or a range. Not a precise 0–100 that the data cannot support.
- The breakdown names the factors that actually matched, and the ones that did not.
- When the history is thin, say the range is wide. Do not invent a tighter number.
- Show the past posts it is most similar to, with their real performance. If there are not three, say so.
- After a post has real metrics, record whether the call was directionally right. Show that record. Do not grade a post that has no metrics yet.
- An optimize button may suggest a small change drawn from a pattern that is already clear. If nothing is clear, it stays quiet.
- The score can be hidden.

## 4. Content Series / Formats Library

Proven structures that can be reused with a new topic.

- Suggest a format only when the posts already show it repeating and the gap is real. Examples of the shape, not a requirement to invent them: myth against reality, before and after, three mistakes, a day, a customer story, a short tip with a CTA.
- A format can also be saved by hand: structure, section order, hook pattern, media note, and example posts.
- Stats per format only when the uses are real. Average, best instance, and how often, with the counts.
- One click applies a format plus a new topic into the composer, with the voice constraints.
- Tag and search them.
- Suggest retiring or refreshing a format only when its later uses are actually behind the earlier ones, with three on both sides and a real gap.
- Link a format to Ideas and to the Calendar.

## 5. Feedback Buttons

Explicit signals on suggestions, so judgment is not only the eventual post performance.

- On a caption, hook, rewrite, idea, hashtag set, or format recommendation: Used, Better, Worse, Off-brand, Irrelevant.
- An optional short note.
- Store it against the suggestion type and the context: brand, topic, format.
- The Memory panel shows the totals. "Marked off-brand" is a count, not a score.
- When explicit feedback conflicts with a performance pattern, the feedback wins until it is corrected.
- Past feedback can be reviewed and corrected.

## 6. Cross-Brand Insights

Optional. Last. Only after one brand's loop is solid.

- A separate view. Aggregated. No voice, no hook line, and no message crosses from one brand to another.
- Comparisons are about type, time, and format, and only when both sides have the posts and the gap is real. Do not invent the "X%".
- Opt in per brand. Easy to turn off entirely.
- A cross-brand line, if it is ever offered to the composer, is labeled as cross-brand. It is never applied to the wrong brand by accident.

Do not build this while a single brand's history, memory, and corrections are still unfinished.
